# Authored for the boundary of the hexagon. The vocabulary is deliberately
# unconventional: what makes an interface a port is who writes it and who calls
# it, never the word its name ends with or the package it sits in.

# way out is the position it holds: a type of the core keeps it as a collaborator, and no

#
# The caller itself stays undecided. Its single final field reads as a value and as an
# identity at once, neither wins, and what would settle it - being the pivot between a port
# and the domain - belongs to the wave after this one.
