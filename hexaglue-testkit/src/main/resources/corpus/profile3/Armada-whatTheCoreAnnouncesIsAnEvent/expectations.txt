# Armada-whatTheCoreAnnouncesIsAnEvent
#
# A one-way contract, and the immutable news the core hands it. Nothing is called an event,
# a publisher or a listener.

# What the interface is for is read from the shape of its signatures: one method, returning
# nothing, taking a type of the application that cannot be changed once made. A way out that
# only ever accepts and never answers is announcing rather than storing or asking, and what
# it is handed is therefore news about the domain.
#
# The counter-case lives elsewhere in the corpus and matters here: a marker interface, with no
# caller and no method, announces nothing and stays undecided. Being immutable is not enough
# to be news; being handed to something whose whole trade is one-way is.
status: reviewed
expect: com.acme.armada.yard.Trumpets = DRIVEN_PORT
expect: com.acme.armada.yard.FleetLaunched = DOMAIN_EVENT
expect: com.acme.armada.yard.Herald = APPLICATION_SERVICE
